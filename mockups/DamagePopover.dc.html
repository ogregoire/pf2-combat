Damage popover — the type row only appears when it changes the number

Shown for the two cases as they actually occur in the data. 838 of 1450 creatures carry some immunity, weakness or resistance — but many of those are condition immunities (disease, paralyzed, unconscious) that damage type cannot affect, so the row keys off damage-type entries only.

CASE A nothing to choose
Dovan from Nisroch 18/30
No immunities, weaknesses or resistances — damage type is irrelevant here.
The GM types 14, presses Damage. No decision, no extra click — which is the common case at the table.
CASE B only the types this creature actually cares about
Skeletal Tiger Lord 99/99
AC 28
DAMAGE TYPE — 7 RELEVANT
None mental IMM poison IMM cold 10 electricity 10 fire 10 piercing 10 slashing 10
Bludgeoning is absent — a skeleton doesn’t resist it, so there is nothing to pick. Hitting it with a mace is just “None”.
The selection resets to None after every Damage. It is per-push, never sticky — so the next hit can’t silently inherit the last one’s type. Heal never shows the row at all.
